AI Language Contributor Program

QuantumCoders Data Solutions (QCDS) is expanding its contributor network for upcoming AI and language technology projects. Through our collaboration with Karya Pvt. Ltd., we are onboarding contributors for language-focused projects that help build advanced AI systems for Indian languages.

Participant Contribution Areas

Speech Data Collection

Record voice clips read from scripts in target dialects under clean audio conditions.

Language Validation

Verify grammar, spelling, pronunciation, and accuracy of generated language samples.

Transcription

Convert spoken audio files into precise textual formats with speaker label identifiers.

AI Data Annotation

Tag text datasets with entities, intent categories, sentiment labels, and annotations.

Language Technology Research

Assist in analyzing linguistic patterns, morphological structures, and validation paradigms for LLM models.

Project LokVaani Overview

Language dataset building initiative:

  • Objective: Collect and preserve diverse Indian regional languages & accents.
  • AI Training: Build a large-scale dataset to train speech recognition, translation, and NLP systems.
  • Inclusivity: Promote digital inclusion by making services accessible to regional speakers.
View Guidelines

Phone a Friend Validation Guidelines

Onboarding manual for audio validation tasks:

  • Steps: Karya App > Menu > Refresh > Listen carefully & completely > Answer questions.
  • Payout Rate: ₹5 per minute of validated speech.
  • Validation Checks: Verify 2 speakers, check language match, speaker gender, conversation quality.
  • Rejection Rules: Reject if personal identifiers are mentioned, inaudible (>90%), high noise, abusive language, >2 speakers, or mentioning "KARYA".
