 QCDS

Odia Language Contributor Program

QuantumCoders Data Solutions (QCDS) is recruiting experienced Odia Transcription Contributors for upcoming Language Technology, Data Annotation, and AI-related projects. Contributors may have opportunities to work on projects managed through QCDS and its industry collaborators.

Language Contributor Roles

Audio Transcription

Convert Odia audio recordings into accurate text while following project-specific guidelines and quality standards.

Subtitling & Timestamping

Create and synchronize subtitles using transcription tools such as Subtitle Edit, Aegisub, or similar platforms.

Quality Review

Review completed transcripts for spelling, grammar, formatting, punctuation, and transcription accuracy.

Project Delivery

Complete assigned tasks within project timelines while maintaining required quality standards.

 Language & Skills

Language & Skills Requirements

We are looking for contributors who possess strong linguistic abilities and technical competency in Odia.

Priority Language
Odia

Fluent reading, writing, and comprehension skills in Odia. Native speakers with high commitment to transcription quality.

We are looking for contributors who possess:

Fluent reading, writing, and comprehension skills in Odia
Strong listening and typing abilities
Minimum 1–2 years of audio transcription or subtitling experience
Experience using Subtitle Edit, Aegisub, or similar tools
Attention to detail and commitment to quality
 Qualifications

Eligibility Criteria

Ensure you satisfy the following project criteria before submitting your application:

Minimum age: 18 years

Must be at least 18 years of age with valid ID proof.

1–2 Years Experience

Minimum 1–2 years of transcription or subtitling experience.

Laptop or Desktop

Must have a computer with a stable, reliable internet connection.

Familiarity with Software

Familiarity with transcription and subtitle editing software like Subtitle Edit or Aegisub.

Deadline Oriented

Ability to meet project deadlines and quality requirements.
