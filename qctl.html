 What We Deliver

Our Professional Services

Every service is executed by trained specialists with rigorous quality control, delivering results that meet enterprise standards and pass Super QC audits consistently.

Audio Transcription
Accurate speech-to-text conversion of audio files with full segmentation and timestamping. We handle diverse accents, noisy environments, and fast speech with consistent accuracy above 99%.
Clean Transcription Verbatim Intelligent Timestamped Multi-speaker
  • Audio segmentation included in every job
  • Speaker diarization for multi-participant audio
  • Filler word tagging and noise annotation
  • Delivered in TXT, DOCX, SRT, or client-specified format
  • Two-stage review: Transcriber → Reviewer → QC
  • 15+ transcribers, 12 min/person/day minimum output
Data Annotation
Structured labeling and tagging of audio, text, and speech data to build machine learning datasets for NLP, ASR, and AI training pipelines. We follow your annotation guidelines precisely.
NLP Labeling ASR Datasets Entity Tagging Sentiment Intent Marking
  • Intent and entity annotation for NLP training
  • Audio quality labeling (clean / noisy / unclear)
  • Emotion and sentiment tagging
  • Dialect and accent classification
  • Custom taxonomy as per client specifications
  • Consistent inter-annotator agreement checks
Voice Over
Professional voice over recording in multiple Indian languages by trained native speakers. Ideal for AI speech dataset collection, e-learning content, IVR systems, and product localization.
Hindi English Odia Studio Quality TTS Datasets
  • Native speakers for each target language
  • Consistent tone, pace, and pronunciation quality
  • Ideal for TTS model training datasets
  • Script reading: spontaneous and prompted speech
  • WAV / MP3 delivery at specified sample rates
  • Metadata tagging included with every file
Audio Segmentation
Precise splitting of raw audio files into sentence-level or utterance-level segments with accurate start/end timestamps, suitable for AI training corpora and QA workflows.
Utterance-level Sentence Splits Timestamps Silence Removal
  • Precise start/end timestamp marking
  • Silence and non-speech interval handling
  • Bundled with transcription for seamless workflow
  • Compatible with Audacity, ELAN, custom tools
  • Delivered in JSON, CSV, or client format
  • Reviewed for accuracy before final submission
Review & Quality Control
Dedicated reviewers audit all transcription output against source audio, correcting errors, ensuring style guide compliance, and passing work through our internal Super QC pipeline before delivery.
2-Stage Review Error Reports Style Guide Super QC Ready
  • 5 dedicated reviewers, 35 min output each/day
  • Transcriber-level error rate tracking per worker
  • Client feedback loops with correction tracking
  • Style guide adherence for every project
  • Rejection and rework tracking in monthly reports
  • Final QC-passed files only sent for delivery
Bulk & Long-Term Data Projects

 Our Workflow

How We Deliver Quality

A structured, multi-stage pipeline ensures every audio minute delivered passes our internal quality gate before reaching your team.

01
📥
Receive Audio Batch
Client or DesiCrew uploads audio files. Team Lead Prem distributes to transcribers based on language and workload capacity.
02
✂️
Segmentation
Specialists segment raw audio into clean utterances with accurate timestamps, marking silence and non-speech regions.
03
📝
Transcription
15+ trained transcribers convert speech to text. Each delivers minimum 12 minutes of approved output per day.
04
🔍
Internal Review
5 dedicated reviewers audit every transcript against source audio. Each delivers minimum 35 minutes of reviewed output per day.
05
✅
Super QC Submission
Reviewed files are submitted to the DesiCrew Super QC team. Only approved minutes are counted for payment.
06
📊
Report & Payment
Monthly performance reports shared with all team members. Payment issued based on Super QC-approved audio minutes.
