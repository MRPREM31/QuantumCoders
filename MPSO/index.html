About MPSO 1.0

In the contemporary professional landscape, a resume lists your past, but a portfolio showcases your potential.

Showcase Your Quantum Potential

This initiative is not merely a competition; it is a developmental opportunity. We invite aspiring developers, designers, and innovators to construct and deploy a creative personal portfolio.

This asset will serve as a testament to your technical proficiency and creative vision, providing a tangible advantage in your future professional endeavors.

Participants are encouraged to leverage modern technologies and are permitted to utilize Artificial Intelligence tools to enhance their creativity and execution. The objective is clear: distinguish yourself from the crowd.

Guidelines & Rules

Please read the following guidelines carefully before participating

Eligibility & Submission Guidelines

Eligibility

Open to all students pursuing undergraduate or postgraduate degrees across India, regardless of their branch or year of study.

Team Structure

This is an individual participation event. Inter-college and inter-specialization restrictions do not apply as entries are individual.

Submission Format

Participants must submit a live, publicly accessible URL of their portfolio website deployed on platforms like Vercel, GitHub Pages, Netlify, etc.

AI Usage

The use of AI tools (for coding assistance, content generation, or design inspiration) is permitted and encouraged to maximize creativity.

Originality

While AI tools are allowed, the final portfolio must represent the participant's own professional identity. Plagiarism will lead to disqualification.

Responsiveness

The website should be responsive and viewable on both desktop and mobile devices. Must contain About, Projects, and Contact sections.
