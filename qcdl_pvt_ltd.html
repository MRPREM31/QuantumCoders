 What We Deliver

Our Professional Services

Every service is executed by trained specialists with rigorous quality control, delivering results that meet enterprise standards and pass Super QC audits consistently.

Audio Transcription
Speech-to-text transcription with segmentation, speaker labeling, and timestamping according to project guidelines and client requirements.
  • Clean, verbatim, and timestamped transcription
  • Multi-speaker and segmentation support
  • TXT, DOCX, SRT or custom formats
  • Review and QC workflow before delivery
AI Data Collection
We provide data collection services for AI and machine learning projects, including voice recordings, image data, and video data collection according to project guidelines and dataset requirements.
  • Voice data collection in multiple languages
  • Image and video data collection projects
  • Metadata and labeling support
  • Suitable for AI training datasets
Data Annotation
Structured labeling and annotation of audio and text datasets for AI and machine learning training based on client guidelines.
  • NLP and speech dataset annotation
  • Intent, entity, and sentiment labeling
  • Audio quality and metadata tagging
  • Quality review and consistency checks
Voice Over
Multilingual voice recordings for speech datasets, e-learning content, IVR systems, and audio projects.
  • Native speakers in multiple languages
  • Script reading and speech recording
  • WAV / MP3 delivery formats
  • Suitable for speech datasets and IVR
Audio Segmentation
Audio splitting and timestamp alignment into sentence or utterance-level segments for transcription and speech datasets.
  • Start and end timestamp marking
  • Silence and non-speech handling
  • JSON, CSV, or custom output format
  • Reviewed before delivery
Review & Quality Control
Multi-stage review and quality control process to ensure guideline compliance and consistent output before delivery.
  • Review and correction workflow
  • Style guide compliance checks
  • Error tracking and corrections
  • Final QC before delivery
Bulk & Long-Term Data Projects
Support for ongoing and large-volume data projects with dedicated teams, structured workflows, and regular delivery schedules.
  • Dedicated project teams
  • Regular delivery schedules
  • Workflow and project coordination
  • Suitable for long-term projects

 Our Workflow

How We Deliver Quality

A structured, multi-stage pipeline ensures every audio minute delivered passes our internal quality gate before reaching your team.

01
📥
Project & Data Assignment
Client provides project guidelines, datasets, or data collection instructions. Tasks are assigned to team members based on language and project requirements.
02
🗂️
Data Preparation
Raw audio, image, or text data is prepared, segmented, or organized according to project workflow before annotation or transcription begins.
03
📝
Annotation / Transcription
Team members perform transcription, labeling, annotation, or data collection tasks following project guidelines and annotation instructions.
04
🔍
Review & Quality Check
Completed work is reviewed by internal reviewers to check guideline compliance, formatting, and annotation consistency.
05
✅
Final Quality Control
Files pass through final quality control before delivery to ensure dataset consistency and project requirements are met.
06
📤
Delivery
Final datasets, transcripts, or annotated files are delivered in the required format according to project specifications.
